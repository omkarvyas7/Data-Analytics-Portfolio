# Stakeholder Communication Plan

Project: Returns Disposition Defect Reduction (DMAIC). Data is synthetic.

## Stakeholder map

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | What they need from us | Approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project Sponsor (Director, Returns Operations) | High | High | Progress vs goal, decisions needed, $ impact | Manage closely: bi-weekly 30-min review, one-page narrative |
| Returns Operations Managers (4 sites) | High | High | What changes on the floor, when, and why | Manage closely: weekly ops review, site walk-throughs |
| Returns QA Manager | High | Medium | Audit design, cause codes, control plan ownership | Core team, daily during pilot |
| ReCommerce Program Manager | High | Medium | Recovery $ and waste trend, open-box rules | Keep satisfied: monthly review |
| Finance partner | Medium | High | Benefit sizing method and assumptions | Keep satisfied: benefit review at Analyze and Control tollgates |
| Software Development Engineer | Medium | Medium | Clear requirements for photo step and checklist fields | Requirements doc, sprint check-ins |
| Learning & Development | Medium | Medium | New-hire defect data, training content needs | Monthly sync |
| Site shift leads and associates | High | Low | Simple instructions, feedback on their shift's quality | Keep informed: start-of-shift huddles, posters, weekly shift scorecard |

## Communication calendar

| Communication | Audience | Owner | Frequency | Channel | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kick-off | All stakeholders | Project Lead + Sponsor | Once (12 Jan 2026) | Meeting | Charter, problem, goal, timeline, roles |
| Tollgate review | Sponsor, site managers, Finance | Project Lead | End of each DMAIC phase | Meeting + 2-page doc | Phase findings, decisions, next steps |
| Weekly status | Core team | Project Lead | Weekly | Email (5 bullets) | Done, next, risks, asks |
| Pilot daily stand-up | RC-CENTRAL ops, QA | Returns Operations Manager | Daily during pilot | 15-min floor huddle | Yesterday's audit results, issues with checklist / photo step |
| Weekly quality scorecard | Site managers, shift leads | Returns QA Manager | Weekly (Monday) | Dashboard + PDF email | p-chart, Pareto, site/shift RAG |
| Executive summary | Sponsor and leadership | Project Lead | Monthly | 1-page narrative | Defect rate, recovery $, waste, decisions needed |
| Close-out and hand-off | All stakeholders | Project Lead + Returns QA Manager | Once (30 Jun 2026) | Meeting + DMAIC report | Results, control plan, owner sign-off |

## Key messages by audience

- Leadership: the defect rate fell from 6.8% to 3.0%. At current volume that protects about $98,483 a year in recovery value and avoids about 435 wasted units.
- Site managers: three changes (SOP v3.0 grading matrix, checklist, photo step) and one weekly chart to watch.
- Associates: clearer rules for open-box and electronics. The checklist is a job aid, not a performance test.
- Finance: the benefit is the audited defect-rate change times the incremental leakage per defect ($16.90), sized on synthetic data.

## Escalation path

A site issue goes to the Returns Operations Manager the same day. If it is still open after 24 hours it goes to the Project Lead, and the Project Sponsor makes a decision within 3 business days.

## Feedback loop

Shift leads get a short pulse survey after weeks 2 and 6 of the pilot. Results and the actions taken go into the next tollgate review.
